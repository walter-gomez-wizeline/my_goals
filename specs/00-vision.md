# Vision and scope — v0.6

## Problem

I need to capture different aspects of my life and revisit them to understand what happens, what I do, what changes, and what deserves my attention. Not everything is a goal, task, or measurement.

## Expected outcome

Capture first, then organize or measure later. The central journey is: capture → organize → consult → reflect → decide what comes next.

Initial persona: the owner of a personal journal. Confirmed language: English for documentation and the interface; user content remains in its original language. Initial timezone: America/Bogota, configurable. Confirmed: responsive web application and an account with shared data across devices. Stack and provider remain undecided.

## Methodological foundation

The adaptation draws on the [official Bullet Journal website](https://bulletjournal.com/blogs/faq): brief task, event, and note entries; index, collections, and daily/monthly/future logs. It also uses [intentional migration](https://bulletjournal.com/pages/migration) to reconsider pending items and choose their destination.

Product extensions include explicit goal metrics, quantitative records, progress views, subtasks, an in-progress state, and optional weekly reviews. These are not presented as requirements of the original method.

## Scope by layer

First functional delivery of the method: a journal with free-form task, event, and note entries; navigable index; collections; future and monthly logs; review and intentional migration. Account, persistence, and history are included from the start.

Optional product extensions, planned after the core: goals with an intention, subtasks and an in-progress state, structured records, tables, and indicators/charts. None may prevent free-form capture or require goal configuration before using the journal.

Gym, food/weight, certification, reading, and ideas are examples. No mandatory category determines what can be recorded. A collection can mix notes, tasks, events, and structured records.

Outside the initial scope: teams, AI, nutrition catalogs, automatic calorie calculation, diet prescriptions, course-provider integrations, external certificate verification, arbitrary formulas, unlimited task nesting, offline synchronization, notifications, and automatic recurrence.

## Discovery decisions

- D-01 resolved: responsive web application for mobile and desktop.
- D-02 corrected: journal and collections are the core. A goal or indicator is not required to capture, organize, or review.
- D-03 access resolved: account with shared data; online operation is the initial proposal.
- D-04: daily, monthly, and future perspectives belong to the method; configurable indicator periods become available with that extension.
- D-05: retain optional support for increasing/decreasing observations and milestone progress without making them the universal structure.

## Success signals

The owner can write a note in an empty journal without creating a goal or collection, find it later, organize it without duplication, and review pending items without losing history. Reflection or records can reveal progress without percentages. Extensions must support weight/course examples without mandatory gym fields.
